Capturing $300K in liquidator value in two weeks.

The live page Moonwell uses to prove a governance upgrade is paying for itself, in real time, on Base and Optimism.

ProtocolMoonwell
ChainsBase · Optimism
EngagementStrategic Data Partnership
StatusLive

The brief

Moonwell shipped a governance upgrade that changed how the protocol captures and distributes OEV, the revenue surfaced when a position is liquidated. Contracts on-chain, audit clean, ready to earn.

The team had no way to see whether it was working.

“We can read the contracts, but we can’t read the protocol. Build us the surface that proves the upgrade is paying for itself, or isn’t.”

What we built

A live page on Atlas that decomposes every liquidation on Base and Optimism into the four flows that matter.

Revenue captured

What the protocol keeps from each liquidation.

Incentives paid

What goes to the liquidators doing the work.

Net OEV

Per market, per chain, per day.

Liquidator concentration

Who is doing the work, and who is getting paid.

Behind it: normalised liquidation events from our archive nodes, a per-market revenue model, and alerts when concentration crosses a healthy threshold.

The numbers, first 14 days

$14K+ Revenue captured

Routed to the Moonwell treasury via the upgraded contracts.

$300K+ Liquidator incentives

Paid out to liquidators, keeping the market healthy.

2weeks Time to insight

From a shipped contract to proof it works.

Why it mattered

Events span two chains, several markets, and a flow that splits at the contract level. Without a purpose-built surface the team would have known the upgrade shipped. They would not have known if it was working.

The page turns the upgrade into continuous, public proof: every liquidation, every payout, on one surface the team and the community check daily.
