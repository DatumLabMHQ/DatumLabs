Collateral File

The file a risk team wants to read before they vote on your token.

Five working days. One written file, one live asset page, and a person who answers when the forum pushes back.

A listing is won in the risk review, not the forum post.

Risk teams are asked to accept an asset they did not issue, on numbers they cannot check.

Most listing requests give them a paragraph of narrative and a price chart. So the request sits, gets a cautious cap, or gets refused without a reason anyone can act on.

We answer their four questions in writing, with the query behind each figure and the date it was true, then stand up a live asset page carrying the same numbers so they can watch the asset instead of taking our word for it once.

What they actually ask

YieldWhere it comes from, and who pays it
StressWhat happens to the price
ExitHow deep, and on which venue
ControlWho can change the terms, and how fast

A file, a page, and someone on the thread.

The file

Twelve to twenty pages: yield source, redemption mechanics, depth and slippage by venue, concentration, governance surface, and the parameters we would argue for.

The parameter ask

A specific proposal: LTV, liquidation threshold, supply cap, oracle choice, and the reasoning for each. A number we will defend.

The live asset page

Hosted by us, rebuilt hourly in Atlas. Supply, borrow, concentration, depth and peg, with the time of the last run.

The forum defence

We are on the thread through the review. Disputed figures get a written reply the same working day, both numbers shown, the gap named.

The correction promise

If we get a figure wrong we correct it within one working day and post the correction wherever the original went.

What we will not do

We will not write a file that argues for a number we do not believe. If the asset is not ready we will say so, and what would make it ready.

Five working days.

Day 1

Kickoff

We agree the target markets and the parameters you want. Bring your docs, contracts and the venues you care about. One hour of your time.

Day 2–3

Modelled

We model the asset in Atlas and build the live page: yield source, depth, concentration, peg behaviour under stress. Nothing needed from you.

Day 4

Draft

Draft file to you with the parameter ask. One round of comments, on a call if you want it.

Day 5

Final

Final file, live page published, forum post drafted. We stay on the thread from here.

Assumes your documentation exists and someone on your side can answer questions inside a day. If the asset is new and the history is thin, we will tell you on the kickoff call rather than at the end.
