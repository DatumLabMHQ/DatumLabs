Atlas

The pipeline behind every number on this site.

Eight protocols, fourteen chains, rebuilt from the chain every hour. Client accounts, live pages and public research all come off the same model, so they cannot disagree.

One model, not a folder of queries.

Atlas is a standing model. Contracts are decoded, markets are mapped and revenue is attributed on a schedule, so a close takes five working days and every figure traces to its run.
