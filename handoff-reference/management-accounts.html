Management Accounts

Your accounts, closed every month, by working day five.

What you earned, what you paid out, what you spent on incentives, what you kept. By chain and by market, with the reason behind each move.

Nobody adds it up.

Three numbers, three places, no reconciliation.

Your protocol earns interest, pays most of it out to suppliers, and spends tokens to bring the deposits in. So “did we make money on Base last quarter?” gets a guess, and your forum gets whatever the loudest tracker says.

We add it up monthly, by chain and by market, then read it: which markets carry you, which cost you money, and what last month’s rewards actually bought once they stopped.

The one page, every month

EarnedInterest, by market
Paid outSuppliers’ share
SpentIncentives, by campaign
KeptThe net

A page you log into, and a report that reads it.

The monthly pack

One page of numbers, two to four pages of reading. Movement on the month named and explained.

By chain, by market

Every figure splits to the market that produced it, so you can see which deployment carries you.

Incentives, costed

Every campaign named, with what it cost, what it brought in, and what stayed after the rewards stopped.

The live page

Built with you, rebuilt from the chain every hour in Atlas, with your closed months kept in it.

The reconciliation

Where our figure differs from a public tracker, the gap is shown and the reason named.

A named analyst

One person who knows your protocol, on a monthly call, and reachable on the days in between.

Working day five, every month.

Day 1–2

Pulled and reconciled

The month is pulled and reconciled in Atlas: interest, supplier share, incentive spend, fees, by chain and market.

Day 3

Read and written

Your analyst reads it and writes the movement: what moved, why, and what it means for next month.

Day 4

Reviewed

Internal review. Every figure traced to the run that produced it.

Day 5

Delivered

Pack to you, live page updated, call booked. Final. If it changes after this, it is a correction and we post it.

The page is yours. If you stop working with us it stays up, with your history in it, frozen at the last month we closed.
